About Us

Family-run, community-focused, and proud of every cut.

Our Story

Mokoka Butchery started with a simple idea: give our neighbours quality meat at fair prices, cut with care. Since opening our doors, we have grown into a trusted local butchery known for fresh cuts, homemade boerewors, and marinated specials.

Every morning our butchers prepare the day's stock by hand, so what you see in the display is what you get at the counter.

What We Stand For

Quality

We source from trusted suppliers and check every delivery before it reaches the display.

Hygiene

Clean workspaces, proper cold storage, and food handling that meets safety standards.

Honest Pricing

Clear prices by weight, so you always know exactly what you are paying for.
