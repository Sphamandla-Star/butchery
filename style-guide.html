Logo Rules

The logo is a text mark: Mokoka Butchery.

  • Always use the exact spelling: Mokoka Butchery (capital M, capital B, no hyphen)
  • The gold dot . after the name is part of the logo
  • On dark backgrounds: white text, gold dot
  • On light backgrounds: dark (#2b2b2b) text, gold dot
  • Minimum font size: 1.2rem
  • Do not change the colours or remove the dot
